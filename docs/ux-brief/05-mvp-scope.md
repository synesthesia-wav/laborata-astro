# What we build first — the smallest useful version

> In plain words. What you can do on day one, what comes right with it because you asked for it, and what waits. Every screen listed here must serve one of the walks in the brief for one of the people we named. If it does not, we do not build it now.

---

## The core walks, as screens

### Must be there on day one

**Home — a calm doorway, not a dashboard**

You land and understand at once: *Find the right tests, at a fair price.* You see a big search that understands Romanian with and without the little marks — *tsh, TSH, tsh* all find *TSH*. Below that, a small row of concerns in plain words — *Energy and sleep, Heart, Hormones, Gut, Nutrient gaps, Inflammation* — that leads to the right tests and bundles without needing a code. Further down, two short shelves — *Common tests* and *Common bundles* — and a quiet list of labs. No meter cards, no *Welcome back*, no stock photo of a doctor.

**Search and results — one place you type and see**

You type and see results as you type, with gentle hints. Each result is one clear card with the same name everywhere — *Vitamina D (25-OH)* reads the same whether it came from Synevo or Sante. A card shows the price range and how many labs sell it, and a small *Save* to add it to a list. You can filter by concern, by lab, and by sample type (*Sânge, Urină, Frotiu*), and you can sort plainly. If nothing matches, the page says so kindly and suggests what to try.

**A single test — what it watches, what it costs, what sample you bring**

You open *TSH* or *Vitamina D (25-OH)*. At the top a short sentence says what the test watches — for example *Thyroid check* or *Iron stores* — and what sample is needed — *Sânge*. Under that, a small table shows prices side by side across the five labs — *Sante 110 lei, Synevo 120 lei, Invitro 135 lei* — and under that the honest line for the collection fee — *plus 30 lei for blood draw, once* or *0 lei — included* — shown once, not per test. A small note says when the price was last seen and where it came from. You can save to a list right there. If a lab does not sell it, it says *Not available* in gray, not hidden.

**A bundle — what is inside, plainly**

You open *Profil tiroidian* or *Lipidogramă*. At the top a short, kind explainer says what the bundle watches, who it is often for, and what to read next — this is the quiet companion for the steady watcher, plain words above the table. Under that, a table lists the parts — *TSH, T3 liber, T4 liber, Anti-TPO* — and for each lab that sells a ready bundle, how many parts are inside and what is missing. If a lab does not sell it as a ready bundle, it says plainly *Not yet sold as a bundle here* and does not invent it. You can add all parts at once or one by one.

**Labs and branches — where to go, shown on a map**

You open a lab like *Synevo* or *Sante* and see its branches as a list and on a map. Each branch shows name, address, hours, phone, and what sample types it can take. You can filter by lab and by sample type, and you can search a street or neighbourhood like *Botanica*. The count stays visible — *12 branches match* — and tapping a branch centers the map. If nothing matches, the page says so kindly and suggests widening the filter, not an empty map.

**Your lists — named, kept, and shareable without an account**

You can make a list like *Analizele mele* or *Mama — control anual*, add tests from search or from inside a bundle, make another list without losing the first, and send any list as a simple link that opens for someone else with no account — showing the same prices and the same chosen branch. You can keep up to ten lists, and each shows how many tests are inside.

**Comparison — the honest cheapest place, in one glance**

You add three to five tests and open *Compare*. In one glance you see the cheapest honest place if you go to one lab, with fees included, and a small, calm line that says what you would save if you split and went twice — without pushing you to split. No sponsored ordering inside the table.

### Comes right with it — because you said saving and sharing belong from the start

* **Opening a shared link while not signed in** — the page reads correctly on another phone, shows the same prices and the same branch, in plain words.
* **Seeing a branch's hours as *Open now* or *Opens at 8:00*** — copied to plain text you can paste to family.
* **Coping well on a small phone** — search, filters, tables, and map all work at the small phone size and on a large screen, in light and in dark.

---

### Stays as a quiet note, not a form that pretends

**Booking a visit inside the site.** That needs real agreements with labs and a careful next step. It stays as a calm card: *Soon you will book here. For now, write to hello@laborata.md.* No form that pretends to book.

## What we do not build now — and why

* **A yearly membership or a care team chat** — those belong to the good sites we learned from, not to our honest comparison job.
* **Heavy personalisation at home** — the home stays as search, a small concern row, and two short shelves. We will revisit after the core walks feel quick.
* **Offline lists or heavy history tracking** — lists are named and shareable, with a faint sense of time (*saved now, last seen price here*), but not a full diary yet.
* **Heavy accounts** — lists are kept locally and shared as links, so no one must make an account to be helped.

## How we will draw these screens

We will not draw straight in the product. We will draw in the playground, one screen at a time, with the real parts — not a lookalike — and we will check each of the simple states side by side in one pass:

* when there is nothing, one thing, many things
* when it is loading, when it failed and how you try again, when it is disabled
* when a name is very long or will not break
* when a picture is missing
* when a number is large
* when the list of tests inside a bundle is short or long
* on a small phone and on a large screen, in light and in dark

We give each side-by-side copy a different name so they do not confuse each other, and we write down only what we can actually see — not what we guess from code. After each fix we look again at the same failing state and make sure nothing else broke.

## What we need to say before we draw

This cut keeps price as the headline, with honest fees, and adds plain explanation as a gentle companion for those who watch health steadily. It respects the three people we named, welcomes the quiet fourth who watches over time, and does not turn the site into a membership.

If this smallest useful version feels right, the next step is to draw the home and the single test in the playground, with the real parts, and to look at them at the small phone size and on a large screen, before touching the product.
